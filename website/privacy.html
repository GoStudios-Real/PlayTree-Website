Effective Date: November 21, 2026 • Contact: support@gostudios.com

PLAYTREE is developed by GoConsole Game Studios / GoStudios / PlayTree Studios. This policy explains how information is handled when you use PLAYTREE.

How We Use Information

  • Operate the game, save progress, authenticate accounts, provide support, improve stability/security, and comply with law.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share data only with necessary service/platform providers, for legal/safety reasons, or in a permitted business transfer.

Your Choices

You can delete local save/account files on your device. For privacy requests, email support@gostudios.com.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

Changes
