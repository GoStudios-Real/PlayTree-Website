# PLAYTREE Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 21, 2026  
**Developer/Publisher:** GoConsole Game Studios / GoStudios / PlayTree Studios  
**Game:** PLAYTREE  
**Website:** https://playtree.game  
**Contact:** support@gostudios.com

## 1. Overview
This Privacy Policy explains how PLAYTREE handles information when you use the game, website, or related services. We aim to collect only what is needed to operate the game.

## 2. Information We Collect
**Information you provide:**
- Account information, such as display name, email address, and password, when you create a GoStudios account.
- Support messages you send to us.

**Information stored locally on your device:**
- Game progress, settings, save data, achievements, leaderboards, and account session data may be stored locally on your device.

**Information that may be collected automatically:**
- Basic technical information needed to run the game or website, such as device/platform type, app version, crash/error data, or service logs, if enabled by the platform or hosting provider.

## 3. How We Use Information
We use information to:
- Provide and operate PLAYTREE.
- Save progress and preferences.
- Authenticate accounts and maintain sessions.
- Provide support and respond to requests.
- Improve game stability, security, and performance.
- Comply with legal obligations.

## 4. Sharing of Information
We do **not** sell your personal information. We may share information only when necessary:
- With service providers/platforms that help operate the game, website, or distribution (such as Microsoft, Google, hosting providers), subject to their own terms and policies.
- If required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security.
- In connection with a business transfer, such as a merger or acquisition, where permitted by law.

## 5. Data Storage and Security
We take reasonable measures to protect information. However, no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure. Local save/account files are stored on your device unless a platform/service explicitly syncs them.

## 6. Children's Privacy
PLAYTREE is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at support@gostudios.com and we will review the request.

## 7. Your Choices and Rights
Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information. You can:
- Remove local game data by deleting local save/account files on your device.
- Contact us at support@gostudios.com for privacy requests.

## 8. Third-Party Services
PLAYTREE may be distributed through third-party platforms (Windows, Microsoft Store, Google Play, web hosting). Those platforms may collect information under their own privacy policies.

## 9. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted with a new effective date.
